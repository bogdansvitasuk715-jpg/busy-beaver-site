Questions we hear a lot

How do you price a job?

Every job is a little different, so we price it after we see it. Send photos and a short description and we'll reply with a clear price. You'll know the cost before we start, and there's no charge for the estimate.

How soon can you come out?

We reply to every request within 1 business day. Once you're happy with the price, we'll offer you the next open time that works for your schedule.

Are you insured?

Yes, Busy Beaver Handyman is insured. Ask us and we'll gladly send proof of coverage before the job.

What jobs do you take?

Repairs, installs and upkeep around the house: drywall, painting, TV mounting, furniture assembly, doors and locks, lights and fixtures, gutter cleaning and junk removal. If a job needs a specialist, we'll tell you upfront.

Can you do several small jobs in one visit?

Yes, and it's usually the best way to book us. Send the whole list and we'll plan one visit around it.
