# Busy Beaver Handyman: шаблони повідомлень

Готові тексти для Quo (Settings → Snippets / Saved replies) або щоб копіювати вручну.
`[Name]`, `[job]`, `[price]` і т.д. замінюйте перед надсиланням.
Тексти англійською, бо клієнти американці. Короткі, бо SMS.

---

## 1. Автовідповіді (Quo → Automations / Call flow)

**Missed call (пропущений дзвінок)**
> Hi, this is Busy Beaver Handyman! Sorry we missed your call, we're on a job. Text us what you need fixed (photos help!) and your address, and we'll get back to you shortly. Free estimate: busybeaverky.com

**After hours (неробочий час)**
> Thanks for reaching Busy Beaver Handyman! We're closed right now, but we'll reply first thing in the morning. Send photos and your address so we can prepare your estimate.

**First text (перше SMS від нового клієнта)**
> Thanks for texting Busy Beaver Handyman! What do you need fixed, and what town are you in? A couple of photos helps us give you a price fast.

---

## 2. Нова заявка (з сайту, дзвінка, Thumbtack)

**Перша відповідь на заявку з сайту**
> Hi [Name], this is [Your name] with Busy Beaver Handyman. Thanks for your request for [job]! I'm looking it over now. Could you send 2–3 photos and your address? Then I'll get you a price.

**Попросити фото**
> Could you send a few photos of the area? One close-up and one from a few feet back works best. A tape measure in the photo helps too.

**Уточнити деталі**
> Quick question so I can price it right: [question]?

**Не наш профіль (електрика, сантехніка, дах)**
> Thanks for thinking of us! [Job] needs a licensed [electrician / plumber / roofer], so it's not something we take on. I'd rather tell you upfront than waste your time. If you have any other projects around the house, we'd be glad to help.

**Поза районом обслуговування**
> Thanks for reaching out! Unfortunately [town] is outside our service area right now. We cover Lexington, Nicholasville, Georgetown, Versailles, Winchester, Richmond and Paris.

---

## 3. Ціна й запис

**Ціна**
> Hi [Name], here's your estimate for [job]: $[price]. That includes [materials / labor / haul-away]. Want to get it on the schedule? I have [day] or [day] open.

**Ціна з новою знижкою**
> Hi [Name], your estimate for [job] is $[price]. As a new customer you get $50 off, so your total is $[price minus 50]. Want me to book you in?

**Підтвердження запису**
> You're all set for [day, date] between [time window]. I'll text you when I'm on the way. Reply here if anything changes.

**Нагадування за день**
> Hi [Name], just a reminder that Busy Beaver Handyman is coming tomorrow, [day] between [time window], for [job]. See you then!

**Виїхав**
> Hi [Name], I'm on my way and should be there in about [XX] minutes.

**Запізнююся**
> Hi [Name], I'm running about [XX] minutes behind on my last job. Sorry for the wait, I'll be there as soon as I can.

---

## 4. Після роботи

**Робота завершена**
> All done! Thanks for having Busy Beaver Handyman over, [Name]. If anything doesn't look right, just text me and I'll come back and fix it.

**Прохання про відгук (через 1–2 години після роботи)**
> Hi [Name], thanks again for choosing Busy Beaver Handyman! If you were happy with the work, a quick Google review would mean a lot to a small local business: [Google review link]. Thank you!

**Нагадування про відгук (через 3–4 дні, один раз)**
> Hi [Name], hope everything's holding up great! If you get a minute, we'd really appreciate a review: [Google review link]. Thanks!

---

## 5. Повторні клієнти

**Сезонне нагадування (раз на сезон, тільки клієнтам, що погодились)**
> Hi [Name], it's Busy Beaver Handyman. Fall is a great time to clean gutters and seal drafts before winter. Want us to stop by? Reply STOP to opt out.

**Немає відповіді на ціну (через 2–3 дні, один раз)**
> Hi [Name], just checking in on the estimate for [job]. Happy to answer any questions or adjust anything. No pressure!

---

## Важливо для SMS у США

- Маркетингові SMS (сезонні пропозиції, акції) надсилайте лише тим, хто погодився їх отримувати, і з «Reply STOP to opt out».
- Відповіді на заявки й повідомлення про запис можна надсилати без окремої згоди, бо клієнт сам звернувся.
- Посилання на Google-відгук з'явиться після верифікації Google Business Profile: Google Business → «Попросити відгуки» → копіювати посилання.
- Не обіцяйте знижку за відгук: Google це забороняє.
